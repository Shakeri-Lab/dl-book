Scope and caveats

These particular orthogonal factors are rotations, although SVD factors may include reflections. Paired vector signs can differ from the static plot without changing the map. Repeated singular values also permit a different basis within their shared subspace. The endpoint removes one direction, not every input point. Its errors are matrix-norm errors, not a guarantee of denoising or unchanged predictions. Rectangular SVD also changes coordinate dimensions.

Check yourself. Which unit input is stretched the most, and how long is its image? What does the rank-one map do to the other marked direction?

The first right singular direction (the circle marker): its image has length 3. The rank-one map sends the other marked direction to zero, an error of exactly 1 — the singular value that was discarded.

Transcript and keyboard help
  1. The input unit circle is shown on a fixed, equal-unit ruler. Predict which direction will stretch most and what keeping one direction removes.
  2. A circle marker and a diamond identify two perpendicular input singular directions. The first factor, V transpose, turns them onto the coordinate axes; an unmarked circle would hide this operation.
  3. The aligned circle still has unit radii. The diagonal factor then scales the first coordinate by three and the second by one.
  4. The intermediate image is an ellipse. The left factor U turns its scaled axes into their final output directions without changing their lengths.
  5. The completed map has semiaxis lengths three and one. Both input directions survive.
  6. Keep a dashed copy of the full ellipse, then remove the contribution from the shorter singular direction.
  7. The rank-one image is the long-axis segment. The diamond, which tracks the second input direction, reaches the origin; other inputs retain their component along the first direction.
  8. The omitted second direction has length one. With only one omitted singular value, the operator and Frobenius errors both equal one. This is a matrix approximation result, not evidence that the removed direction is noise.
