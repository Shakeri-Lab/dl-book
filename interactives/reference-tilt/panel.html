Scope and caveats

Smaller \(\beta\) increases expected proxy reward and measured KL here, but individual nonmaximal responses need not gain probability at every setting. Neither the score nor the stay-near dial establishes truth or safety. The slider changes an optimization setting, not a learned model parameter; probabilities are recomputed after subtracting the largest log-weight.

Check yourself. Push β toward zero. Where does the policy go, and how large can the KL from the reference become?

All of the probability moves to D, the response with the highest proxy score, and the KL approaches −ln 0.05 ≈ 2.996. Expected proxy reward tops out at 3. Nothing about the proxy has improved; the policy has only stopped listening to the reference.
