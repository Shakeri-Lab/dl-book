Watch the mechanism: zero is not a padding mask

Does giving padding a score of zero exclude it?

Follow query 2 of the audit's second example. These are its existing four keys, with the intermediate scores made visible.

\( \class{mbs-probability}{\predictionpart{\alpha_j}}=\dfrac{\class{mbs-numerator}{\exp(s_j)}}{\class{mbs-denominator}{\sum_k\exp(s_k)}} \) \( \class{mbs-zero}{\exp(0)=1} \) \( \class{mbs-exclude}{\exp(-\infty)=0} \)

Scope and caveats

The formula uses the scores currently shown. Setting padded scores to zero is deliberately wrong; negative infinity excludes them before normalization. This is source-padding masking, not target-loss or causal masking: all real source keys remain available. Bars share a ruler within each level, but exponentials and weights have different units. An all-masked row is rejected, never treated as four valid zero weights.

Transcript and keyboard help
  1. Query 2 of the second example has four source/key slots. Only the first two represent real keys. Predict whether a score of zero excludes padding.
  2. Try the wrong repair: overwrite both padded scores with zero. Leave the real-key scores unchanged.
  3. Exponentiate each shown score; the four bars grow from their baselines. Each padded slot contributes one, because the exponential of zero is one.
  4. One bracket gathers all four contributions into a shared sum of 3.7233. The two padded slots supply two of it.
  5. Divide every contribution by that sum. Padding receives about 0.5372 of the total weight, even though those source positions do not exist.
  6. Now push the padded scores down from zero toward negative infinity, on the same picture. Their contributions shrink from one to zero, the shared sum falls from 3.7233 to 1.7233 and its bracket closes onto the real keys, and the weight padding held moves onto them.
  7. With the padded scores at negative infinity, the first two weights are 0.4090 and 0.5910; both padded weights are exactly zero. The complete row sums to one.
  8. The guard remains essential: at least one key must be valid. With no valid key, stop before normalization rather than returning an all-zero distribution.
