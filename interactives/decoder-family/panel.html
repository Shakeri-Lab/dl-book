Scope and caveats

The family holds the chapter's two decoders: a = 0 is \( g_1 \) and a = 0.8 is \( g_2 \). The sweep's −0.8 mirrors the chapter's multiple.

At the three codes the term z(z² − 1) is exactly zero, so every member reproduces the targets 1, 0 and 1 with zero reconstruction error; the audit cell below prints a largest observed-code gap of 0.0. At the draw the term is −0.375, so the decoded value is 0.25 − 0.375a, and it keeps moving for as long as a does. Beyond the outer codes the band widens fastest, because the added term grows like z cubed there.

This term is one choice among many: any function that vanishes at the three codes could be added, and reconstruction would not notice. Drawing a new sample needs a distribution over codes, which reconstruction does not supply; Chapter 22 builds one.
