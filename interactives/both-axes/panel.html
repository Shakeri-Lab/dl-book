Scope and caveats

The reordering (by, river, bank, the) is declared. Every token moves, and the reordering does not reverse itself. The chapter's own audit, later in this section, reorders random vectors in another order; the panel takes none of its numbers and none of its order.

The weights are the figure's illustrative ones. Only bank's row is printed, as in the figure; the other rows are shading.

Rows first, then columns, is a drawing device. The rows-only grid is a real quantity: reordered queries scored against keys still in their old order. But attention on the reordered sequence reorders rows and columns at once, as the one-move glide shows.

Had the value blocks moved while the columns stayed put, bank's 0.70 in column 3 would meet the's value block, and bank's output would change. The panel never draws that state.

One example, not a proof. “Bare” means no mask and nothing tied to slot numbers; the chapter adds both later, and each changes more than addresses.
