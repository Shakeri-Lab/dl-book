# Print revisions carried into the HTML edition (2026-10-14 to 2026-10-16)

From October 9 to 14, 2026 the author revised the Springer print edition heavily: a language pass, a
formal-register pass, reviewer edits, seam and attribution fixes, a textbook-delivery audit, and the
author's own edits. The author then asked for this edition to take them: "I just want the language updated
as I believe the book is more polished. I want the interactive animations to stay intact."

**Source.** The print master's text at each file's earliest backup taken after the previous carry (the
October 9 additions) was compared with the master of October 14. Every changed paragraph group is one ledger
entry, 653 in all. A check of the window between the October 8 ledger and the October 9 baseline found only
print-only TikZ drawings and the October 9 additions already carried here.

**The author's decisions (October 16).**
1. Carry the language everywhere, including sentences a replay or scene test quotes. Keep the animations
   intact: only the check strings that look for a changed sentence follow it. Those are the fixture literals
   and anchor targets in `interactives/manifest.json`, and the expected strings of the scene tests. No panel,
   player, timeline or visual changed.
2. Leave the figures. No code cell changed, so plotted labels keep their earlier wording.
3. Keep this edition's structure. Print's moves between chapters are not carried.
4. Keep the print wording carried into unsigned `NOVEL: needs sign-off` blocks, markers included, and apply the
   decision sheet's defaults for the smaller items.

**Kept for the animations.** Six sentences keep this edition's wording, because the replay beside them shows
or quotes their content:
- the momentum range $\beta \in [0.9, 0.99]$ (momentum-memory);
- the term "region of confusion" (sgd-zones);
- "BN needs real batches ... unreliable at tiny batch sizes" (batch-ruler);
- "contiguous chunks of roughly 20–50 steps" (detach-cut);
- the tail of the `fig-highway-time` caption (gate-product);
- the spacing of the attention display (both-axes).

**Outcomes of the porting pass:** already 3, applied 482, needs-author 41, partly 79, print-only 48. The author's decisions then settled the needs-author entries: each unit's list "After the author's decisions of October 16" says what was done. "print-only" means:

**Conversions.**

  labels.
- `\cite` became a link on the authors' names, using the print bibliography's DOI or URL.
- `\footnote` became `^[...]`, and `\(...\)` became `$...$`.
- Retitled headings keep their old ids, so print's companion links and the public anchors still resolve.

The contract's part-page sentence cap rises from 16 to 17 for the author's Part IV text. Its Chapter 16
calibration-boundary string follows Exercise 6's new word order.

**Open, for a later pass.**

  `fig-vanishing-time`, `fig-highway-time`, `fig-gan-finite` and `fig-a1-centering`;
