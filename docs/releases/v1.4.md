# Deep Learning: Making It Learnable v1.4

Version 1.4 numbers the interludes as chapters and makes the book read on its own.

- The three interludes are Chapters 7, 11, and 17. Chapters from 7 on print one to three
  higher than in v1.3; file names, labels, anchors, and URLs are unchanged, and the
  Preface's revision note maps every old number to its new one.
- Every chapter reference is a cross-reference, so the printed numbers follow the reading
  order; numbers drawn inside figures, printed in code, and written into the downloadable
  notebooks follow too, and a CI guard fails on a typed chapter number.
- The text both editions share no longer depends on the course it grew from: labels that
  read "non-examinable" read "optional", and the second volume, *Deep Learning: Making It
  Trainable*, is cited as a separate work. The web edition keeps its course
  route and notes for enrolled readers.
- v1.4 also gathers the rolling changes since v1.3: the voice-coherence pass, the Part III
  revision of Chapters 12 and 13 (six requirements, the GRU cell, the masked update, and the
  running translator exercises), and the HTML-only website.
- Printed output is unchanged by the renumbering and the independence pass (137 frozen
  stdout blocks); the source, notebook, HTML, public-anchor, independence, typed-number, voice, and interaction
  audits pass on the release commit, and a local print proof runs 562 pages.

The HTML edition is canonical and is the only edition the website serves; this release
attaches no PDF.
